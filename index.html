Our Heritage

The Taste of Damascus

Authentic Syrian recipes, hand-stacked rotating spits, and warm Levant hospitality.

Welcome to Shawerma El Shami (شاورما الشامي), Temple Terrace’s home for authentic Syrian street food. Every single day, our halal beef and chicken are hand-stacked, marinated in fragrant ancestral Levant spice blends, and slow-roasted on vertical rotating spits until juicy, tender, and lightly caramelized.

We wrap our shawarma inside warm, fresh Saj bread with crisp Syrian pickles, housemade garlic toum whip, and a drizzle of tangy pomegranate molasses — toasted to golden perfection on the flat-top griddle just like the historic shawarma shops of Old Damascus.
