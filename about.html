Our Story

At Shawerma El Shami (شاورما الشامي), we are proud to bring the true soul of Syrian culinary heritage to Temple Terrace and the greater Tampa Bay community.

True shawarma is an art form. Every morning, our halal beef and chicken are hand-stacked, marinated in ancestral spice blends infused with cardamom, sumac, coriander, and allspice, and slow-roasted on vertical rotating spits until juicy and aromatic.

We slice the meat thin to order and wrap it in warm, lightly toasted Saj bread with crunchy pickled cucumbers and turnips, our house-whipped garlic toum, and a drizzle of tangy pomegranate molasses — pressed on a sizzling griddle to achieve that legendary crunch.
