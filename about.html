The Taste of Elshami

Authentic Syrian street food crafted from cherished family recipes, wholesome ingredients, and made fresh every single day right here in Temple Terrace.

Our Story

Welcome to Shawarma Elshami (شاورما الشامي). We believe that great food starts with real, honest ingredients. Every single day, our kitchen preps wholesome, fresh ingredients—from hand-stacked halal meats to scratch-made sauces—ensuring every bite is as nutritious as it is delicious.

Rooted in traditional family recipes passed down through generations, we marinate our beef and chicken in aromatic Levant spices, slow-roasting them on vertical spits. Pair it with our housemade garlic toum, fresh vegetables, and warm Saj bread for a wholesome, feel-good meal you can trust.
